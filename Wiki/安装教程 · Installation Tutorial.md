# 🌸 MCSM-FRP-Market Add Tutorial 🌸
## 🌸 MCSM-FRP-Market 添加教程 🌸

> *跟着步骤走，小白也能轻松搞定喵~ / Follow the steps and even beginners can do it easily nya~*

---

## 📥 Step 1: Download the Market File
### 📥 第一步：下载市场文件

打开你的浏览器，访问下方链接下载 `market.json` 文件：  
Open your browser and visit the link below to download the `market.json` file:

🔗 **[点我下载 market.json](https://gitee.com/yuqian114514/mcsm-frp-market/releases/latest)**
🔗 **[Click to download market.json](https://github.com/yuqian114514/mcsm-frp-market/releases/latest)**

> 👆 在最新 Release 页面里找到 `market.json`，点击下载就好啦，记得保存到找得到的地方喵~  
> 👆 Find `market.json` on the latest Release page, click to download it, and remember to save it somewhere you can find it later nya~

---

## 🔐 Step 2: Log in to the Panel
### 🔐 第二步：登录面板

打开你的 MCSManager 面板，输入账号密码登录进去喵~  
Open your MCSManager panel and log in with your username and password nya~

---

## 🛒 Step 3: Enter the Template Market
### 🛒 第三步：进入模板市场

在面板侧边栏或顶部导航中，找到并点击 **「模版市场」** 
In the panel sidebar or top navigation, find and click **"Template Market"** 

---

## ✏️ Step 4: Open Edit Mode
### ✏️ 第四步：打开编辑模式

点击 **「编辑应用市场」** 按钮，进入市场编辑页面喵~  
Click the **"Edit App Market"** button to enter the market editing page nya~

---

## 🧹 Step 5: Clear Old Templates
### 🧹 第五步：清空旧模板

点击 **「操作」** → 选择 **「清空当前模板」** → 弹出确认框后点击 **「确认」**  
Click **"Operations"** → Select **"Clear Current Templates"** → Click **"Confirm"** when the confirmation dialog appears

> ⚠️ 这一步会清空现有的模板数据，如果有自定义模板记得先备份哦！  
> ⚠️ This step will clear all existing template data. Remember to back up any custom templates first!

---

## 📤 Step 6: Import New Template
### 📤 第六步：导入新模板

1. 点击 **「导入」** 按钮 / Click the **"Import"** button  
2. 选择 **「从文件导入」** / Select **"Import from File"**  
3. 在弹出的文件选择器中，找到并上传刚刚下载的 `market.json` 文件 / In the file picker that appears, find and upload the `market.json` file you just downloaded  
4. 点击 **「保存到面板」** / Click **"Save to Panel"**

---

## 🎉 Done!
### 🎉 完成啦！

导入成功后，你的应用市场就已经更新为最新模板啦～现在可以去愉快地使用 FRP 模板了喵！💖  
Once imported successfully, your app market is updated with the latest templates～ Now you can happily use the FRP templates nya! 💖

> 💡 **小提示**：如果遇到导入失败，请检查 `market.json` 文件是否完整下载，或者尝试重新下载一次喵~  
> 💡 **Tip**: If the import fails, check whether the `market.json` file was downloaded completely, or try downloading it again nya~
